I'm a computational / systems neuroscientist interested in the neural mechanisms of learning. I am completing my PhD from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem under the supervision of [Israel Nelken](https://elsc.huji.ac.il/people-directory/faculty-members/israel-nelken/) and [Yonatan Loewenstein](https://elsc.huji.ac.il/people-directory/faculty-members/yonatan-loewenstein/).

In my PhD I studied how rats rapidly learn a multi-step decision task, using abstract representations and generalization. To study the neural basis of this behavior, I recorded from multiple brain regions (with a focus on the orbitofrontal cortex) in freely moving, untethered rats using chronically implanted Neuropixels probes.

I am especially interested in how neural networks (within and across brain regions) encode information, how these patterns of activity are formed, and how they support and obstruct behavior.
